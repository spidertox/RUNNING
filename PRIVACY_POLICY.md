# Privacy Policy — Running App

**Effective date:** 2026-10-24

## Data we collect
- **Location (GPS)**: Used ONLY while you record a run, in the foreground. Your route is stored **locally on your device**.
- **No account required.** No email, no phone number, no ads, no analytics SDKs.

## What we DO NOT do
- No background location tracking
- No data sale or sharing with third parties
- No cloud upload (sync is optional and off by default)

## Permissions used
- `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` — run tracking (foreground only)
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION` — keep GPS alive during an active run
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — show "Tracking your run" notification
